# Step 7.1: adaptive flush cadence. Baseline is the shipped fixed draw, one
# flush in five rounds; interval-1 is step 6's upper bound on what flushing
# alone can buy on the mesh. stale is the first, purely local rule and
# adaptive the gated one that replaced it -- see design/step7-flush-cadence.md.
# Every variant pins --bucket-policy fixed, the width these were measured with
# before step 7.3 made adaptive bucketing the default, and --idle-flush off,
# the cadence they were measured with before step 7.4 made `starved` the
# default.
fixed --flush-policy fixed --bucket-policy fixed --idle-flush off
interval1 --flush-policy fixed --flush-interval 1 --bucket-policy fixed --idle-flush off
stale --flush-policy stale --bucket-policy fixed --idle-flush off
adaptive --flush-policy adaptive --bucket-policy fixed --idle-flush off
