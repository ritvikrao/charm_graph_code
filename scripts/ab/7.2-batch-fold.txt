# Step 7.2, receiver side: the batch-local fold in the delivery callback, alone
# and on top of the source hold, so the two contributions can be read apart.
# Every variant pins --bucket-policy fixed, the width these were measured with
# before step 7.3 made adaptive bucketing the default, and --idle-flush off,
# the cadence they were measured with before step 7.4 made `starved` the
# default.

fold --combine off --batch-fold on --bucket-policy fixed --idle-flush off
hold --combine hold --batch-fold off --bucket-policy fixed --idle-flush off
hold+fold --combine hold --batch-fold on --bucket-policy fixed --idle-flush off
